# Industry Next field map

Industry Next is the workshop in a larger creative circuit. Its job is to let a visitor do something immediately: play, remix, assemble, publish, or export. It should not become another editorial homepage, identity dashboard, or mint interface.

## The circuit

1. **Industry Next prototypes.** Small public instruments make CC0 participation tangible.
2. **PointCast publishes and distributes.** Editorial context, house campaigns, and public reporting help the strongest experiments travel.
3. **Passport carries identity and provenance.** Authorship, receipts, and reputation become portable only when they improve the experience.
4. **Focused project sites deepen successful worlds.** Repeated behavior earns its own archive, rules, community, editions, and optional collecting rails.
5. **Tezos settles selectively.** Ownership and collecting follow the creative act; they do not gate it.

## Product rules

- One new public instrument at a time.
- Every experiment produces a real output a visitor can keep or share.
- No sign-in or wallet before the first meaningful action.
- Publish actions are explicit, reversible where practical, and clear about what becomes public.
- PointCast measures distribution; Industry Next measures creation and return behavior.
- Promote repeated, loved behavior into a focused site. Retire experiments that do not earn another version.

## Near-term sequence

1. Permission Lab: make, export, publish, and browse.
2. Public prompts: a rotating weekly constraint, distributed by PointCast.
3. Passport receipts: optionally attach authorship after making.
4. Project graduation: give the most repeated form a dedicated world.
