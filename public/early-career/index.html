02 / THE WHOLE OFFER

A job is an operating system.

Ask for the documents. Write down the answers. Compare the lived week, not only the headline number.

01 / CASHWhat is guaranteed?

Base, bonus rules, pay timing, reimbursements, and review dates.

02 / HEALTHWhat can a bad year cost?

Premium, deductible, network, leave, disability, and dependents.

03 / TIMEWho controls the week?

Hours, commute, travel, availability, recovery, and schedule control.

04 / LEARNINGWill your range expand?

Access to decisions, feedback, tools, customers, and consequential work.

Equity terms, vesting, credit, portfolio rights, IP, and revenue share.

Notice, severance, cliffs, noncompetes, references, and portable proof.

Ask a qualified professional about legal, tax, benefit, immigration, or financial terms you do not understand. A checklist is not individualized advice.

03 / FIRST 90 DAYS

Do not spend the first month proving you already know.

Your job is to become useful without becoming invisible. Learn the system, improve one piece, then leave a receipt.

DAYS 01–30

See the real system.

  • Learn the nouns people actually use.
  • Map who decides, who knows, and who gets affected.
  • Ask what “good” looks like and where work usually breaks.
SHIP / A ONE-PAGE SYSTEM MAP
DAYS 31–60

Repair one door.

  • Choose a repeated friction you can observe.
  • Propose a bounded change with an owner and finish line.
  • Test it with the people who use it.
SHIP / ONE BEFORE + AFTER
DAYS 61–90

Make the learning travel.

  • Document what changed and what did not.
  • Name the evidence and the remaining risk.
  • Teach the next person how to repeat or improve it.

04 / AI AT WORK

Keep the human part inspectable.

  1. 01
    Protect the material.

    Do not paste private, client, personnel, or regulated data into a tool unless the organization has explicitly approved that use.

  2. 02
    Verify consequential claims.

    Check facts, calculations, citations, permissions, and generated code at the level the outcome deserves.

  3. 03
    Show your decisions.

    Keep the prompt less interesting than the judgment: what you chose, rejected, changed, and why.

  4. 04
    Own the output.

    “The model said” is not a handoff. You remain responsible for what you send, publish, recommend, or ship.
