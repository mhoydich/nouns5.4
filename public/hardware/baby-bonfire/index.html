00 / WHAT THIS IS

A venture,
run in the open.

  1. 01
    The product

    Small objects for a shelf or a table. Each one brings one element into the room: a flame, a bowl of water, moving air, sand and stone, a light, a sound, a bit of weather. No screen on any of them.

  2. 02
    The status

    Exploration. Started 11 November 2026. So far there are drawings, interactive scenes in build, and a written venture file. No object exists yet.

  3. 03
    The test

    Industry Next runs this with an AI product manager (Claude). The question is how far AI resources can take a physical product toward market. The steps only a person can do are named below.

READ THIS FIRST

Nothing on this page has been manufactured, burn-tested, certified or offered for sale. Every piece named here is a design direction. Where a stage has a gate, the venture does not move until the gate is met.

01 / THE SYSTEM

Every piece
has a place.

One footprint under the whole line. The object changes; the tile it sits on does not.

01 / PIECE

The piece

Any object in the line: a hearth, a bowl, a lamp, a bell. It does its job with nothing plugged in.

The thing you look at
02 / PLACE

The place

A standard palm-size stone tile. Every piece sits on one. The tile carries a tap chip.

The shared footprint
03 / PUCK

The puck

A swappable disc under the place. It senses heat and light, and later air. It has no camera and no microphone.

Swappable
04 / SCENE

The scene

The arrangement of pieces on places is the scene. Move a piece and the scene changes.

The shelf is the interface
FIRE IS THE SWITCH: When the puck senses a flame, a condition is met and the room responds: an air column draws, a lamp drops, a sound starts. Snuff the flame and each of those reverses.

The place footprint is meant to be published, so other makers can build pieces that sit on it. That is stage five, not a promise with a date.

04 / RULES / WHO DOES WHAT

The lines
it stays inside.

These are fixed before any design is. A piece that cannot meet them does not join the line.

  • 01Fully containedAnything meant for indoor use is fully contained.
  • 02No liquid fuelNone, in any piece.
  • 03No health claimsNo health or wellness claims, in copy or on packaging.
  • 04Palo santo, sourcedOnly as Bursera graveolens, naturally fallen, with documents.
  • 05White sage, provenOnly farmed, with proof. Otherwise not used.
  • 06Forms, not symbolsShapes are not religious symbols.
  • 07No borrowed marksNo team names or marks without a license.
  • 08No watchingNo cameras and no microphones, in any piece.
