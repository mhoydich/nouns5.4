A NOTE ON THE COLLECTIVE

Everyone
has some

A collective should not ask for all of you. It should make whatever time you freely offer useful, visible, and enjoyable—and make it easy to leave and return.

01 / THE PREMISE

COME
AS
YOU ARE

Belonging is not a timesheet.

Most collectives quietly reward the person who can stay in every channel, attend every call, remember every decision, and answer immediately. Availability becomes authority. Exhaustion becomes proof of care.

That is not collective intelligence. It is a hidden full-time job with sentimental compensation. The people with jobs, children, illness, distance, changing attention, or simply other loves drift to the edge.

Not everyone has spare time. Everyone has a relationship to time. A decent collective respects that relationship and never presumes access to it.

One useful hour counts. So does choosing not to give it.

03 / THE COLLECTIVE PROTOCOL

Five promises
before the ask.

  1. 01
    TIME IS OFFERED

    No assumed availability, response debt, attendance theater, or urgency inherited from somebody else.

  2. 02
    SMALL COUNTS

    A contribution is designed to finish at its stated size. Nobody must become permanent to become meaningful.

  3. 03
    ENJOYMENT IS EVIDENCE

    Pleasure, learning, friendship, and surprise are legitimate outcomes—not distractions from “real” work.

  4. 04
    CREDIT STAYS VISIBLE

    The artifact keeps the contributor’s name, terms, and context. No points, streaks, or extractive leaderboard.

  5. 05
    RETURN IS NORMAL

    Leaving creates no shame. Re-entry needs a current door, not a complete history lesson.
