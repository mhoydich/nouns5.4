02 / MARKET RULES

Tokens do not
erase terms.

  1. 01
    A listing is an invitation.

    It is not an employment offer, contractor agreement, token sale, or guarantee of selection.

  2. 02
    The first proof stays bounded.

    No candidate owes unpaid commercial production. Any permitted use belongs in writing.

  3. 03
    Reward terms precede work.

    Amount, access, timing, acceptance, tax treatment, and settlement are confirmed in a separate agreement.

  4. 04
    Humans approve settlement.

    No card here connects a wallet, holds escrow, mints a token, or triggers an automatic payout.
