01 / THE ASSIGNMENT

A playlist is
a small world.

Give it a premise, a front door, an arc, a reason for track seven, and a life after launch.

You will build and maintain Spotify playlists for Industry Next projects and selected music we believe deserves a wider room. Each one should feel authored without becoming precious: clear enough to enter, surprising enough to stay, alive enough to revisit.

Growth means more humans choosing to listen—and choosing again. You will connect editorial, visual direction, artist relationships, release rituals, community circulation, and honest measurement into one repeatable practice.

02 / WHAT SITS IN YOUR HANDS

Five faders.
One signal.

  1. 01

    Concept

    Name the promise, audience, setting, and emotional arc before adding a song.

  2. 02

    Sequence

    Research deeply. Build entrances, turns, breath, surprise, and a finish worth replaying.

  3. 03

    Package

    Direct the cover, title, description, notes, credits, and every shareable edge.

  4. 04

    Circulate

    Create release rituals with artists, labels, DJs, communities, editorial, and owned channels.

  5. 05

    Read

    Review what people actually do, document the lesson, and tune the next edition.

05 / THE FIT

Proof over
pedigree.

You may come from radio, record shops, labels, music writing, DJ culture, fan communities, growth, or somewhere with no useful title yet.

You can.

  • Hear an arc, not just collect good tracks
  • Write a title and description people remember
  • Give precise visual direction for covers
  • Build trust with artists and music communities
  • Use data without flattening taste
  • Maintain a playlist after launch day

We never.

  • Buy streams, followers, saves, or comments
  • Use bots, click farms, or listener impersonation
  • Promise editorial placement we do not control
  • Hide pay-for-placement or playlist takeovers
  • Call traffic a community
  • Guarantee a number before earning a listener
