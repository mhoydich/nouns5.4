ABOUT NOUNS / A SHORT THESIS

Permission is the starting point.

Nouns is interesting not only because the characters are memorable. It is interesting because anyone can take them somewhere unexpected—without asking first.

ONE IMAGE
MANY DIRECTIONS

01

THE CC0 IDEA

An image can be a finished work—and public material.

CC0 places permission at the center. People can copy, change, publish, sell, combine, discard, or rediscover the work. The interesting part is not simply that the art is “free.” It is that the next person has the right to surprise the first one.

01 / ACCESS

No permission queue.

An idea can move at the speed of the person who has it.

02 / CULTURE

Circulation adds meaning.

Each new use can make the shared visual language larger, stranger, and more useful.

03 / AUTHORSHIP

The audience can become the studio.

Participation is no longer limited to collecting or commenting. It can produce the next work.

02

WHY NOUNS

A character system built to travel.

Nouns has an unusual balance: simple enough to remix, specific enough to recognize. The square glasses, pixel grammar, bright grounds, and improbable heads make a Noun legible across posters, games, objects, stories, clubs, and things that do not have a category yet.

That makes Nouns a vivid demonstration of CC0. A derivative is not a leak from the system. It is evidence that the system is alive.

THE INTERESTING FRONTIER

“What if an artwork could become more itself each time somebody else made something with it?”

The answer will not come from one official story. It comes from many people trying useful, beautiful, funny, ambitious, and deeply weird things in public.

This page treats Nouns as creative material: open, recognizable, and unfinished on purpose.
