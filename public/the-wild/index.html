01 / THE SMALL BET

One spirit.
One cent.

The native app remains the source of truth for availability, taking, prayer responses, and any future settlement. This field file is a doorway, not a mirror of live state.

03 / DISCOVERY WITHOUT A GROWTH MACHINE

Let the experiment earn its next layer.

We are not building scale machinery in advance. Each next step must answer a question the current layer cannot.

  1. NOW

    Direct field test

    The public app, its machine-readable manifest, and this Industry Next field file make the experiment easy to inspect.

  2. NEXT

    Small distribution

    PointCast, Industry Next, and shareable collectible-style invitations bring in people and agents who enjoy testing new interaction rituals.

  3. ONLY AFTER PROOF

    Agent discovery

    After a verified $0.01 settlement, list the recurring prayer route where unfamiliar agents can discover it. Measure repeat use, not hype.

  4. ONLY IF EARNED

    Careful expansion

    Rate limits, reconciliation monitoring, identity signals, and richer rituals arrive only if real participants make them necessary.
