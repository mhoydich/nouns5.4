FIELD NOTE 004 / CONCEPT BY MICHAEL HOYDICH

El Segundo Motors: a weekend case study in AI as the coordination layer

A weekend starts with a luxury car and ends with a street train. The useful result is a clearer question: can a small fleet move more people, with a service business and a test that tells us when to stop?

01 / THE PIVOT

Less car.
More people mover.

Michael’s account begins with El Segundo Motors as a family of small luxury cars. His next direction changes the unit of design: “less car, more people mover.”

The Segundo Line answers with short electric pods. Each is imagined with its own motor, batteries and job; the cars join into a street train when demand grows and separate when the day quiets down. The point is to match capacity to a place and a time, instead of asking every trip to carry an entire private car.

One pod is a ride.
Eight pods are a train.

This remains a design concept. Its proposed rule is no towed riders: every car would drive, brake and steer itself, with coupling only at hubs. AI would propose the formation; a human dispatcher would approve it. These are design requirements to validate, not demonstrated capabilities or a statement that the system is approved for public roads.

02 / THE OPERATING IDEA

The fleet follows the tide.

The four tides are a proposed service pattern. They make the same set of vehicles useful at different scales, from a beach Saturday to a late ride home.

Ten jobs, one proposed family.

Pilot leads; Open carries riders; Quiver carries boards and bikes; Level provides step-free access; Galley serves food and drink; Hold carries cargo; Bandstand becomes a stage; Tender brings power; Seminar is a classroom for the proposed University of El Segundo; Hush offers a quiet ride.

The names help a studio ask a concrete question of each car: whose trip improves, and what changes operationally? A compelling lineup is still a set of hypotheses until those needs are tested with riders and operators.

03 / THE BUSINESS VIEW

Sell useful service hours.

The proposed customer is an organization with people to move: LAX hotels, aerospace campuses and event organizers.

The business thesis is to sell service hours on a defined route or around a defined event. A hotel might need a repeatable guest connection; a campus might need a short transfer between buildings; an organizer might need capacity before and after a crowded hour. These are prospective uses, not signed customers or partner commitments.

That changes the questions. Can an operator deliver reliable hours? What does each hour cost after staffing, leases, charging, maintenance, insurance and downtime? Which route earns a repeat booking? Does the service reduce a car trip or simply add a novelty ride?

A contractable service and its operating record would come before an expensive custom fleet. The Saturday Tide proposal is a way to learn whether the transport problem is real before building the couplers.

04 / PHASE 0 · SATURDAY TIDE · SUMMER 2027 TARGET

Prove the riders before building the couplers.

The proposal calls for four leased low-speed shuttles operating as a radio-linked virtual train from Smoky Hollow, via Main Street and Grand Avenue, to the beach. Ten Saturdays in summer 2027 would test demand and the operating routine without requiring a new coupling system.

The concept budget is $145k to $260k, including a 20% contingency. It covers vehicle leases, staffing, insurance, counsel and permits, radios and counters, stops and signs, and outreach. It is a planning range, not a quote or authorized expenditure; no vehicles, route permissions or dates are booked by this case study.

Proposed build-or-stop gates

The public microsite proposes three demand tests for the Saturday Tide. These are proposed decision thresholds; the pilot has not produced results.

  1. At least 12 boardings per vehicle-hour.Test whether the vehicles do enough useful work.
  2. Peak loads above 85% on 6 of 10 Saturdays.Test whether more capacity is needed often enough to justify it.
  3. 30% of riders say they would otherwise have driven.Test the intended substitution, with the limits of a rider survey made clear.

Couplers get built only if riders show up and the evidence supports moving forward. If demand falls short, the next decision is to revise the route or service, or stop the custom-vehicle track. Before any pilot, operators would still need to resolve the route, accessibility, permissions, insurance and safety requirements. Passing a demand gate would not itself authorize a coupled service.

05 / HOW MICHAEL DESCRIBES THE WORK

A guild of models.
One human direction.

In Michael’s account, Claude Opus 5.5 orchestrated the work, with Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Haiku 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1 threads, ChatGPT image generation and Codex.

He puts the Claude spend at about $67 at list prices. That is his estimate for the Claude work, not an independently audited bill, a price comparison, or the cost of delivering an actual transport service. Model names, orchestration details and spend are reported here as his account.

The case study’s lesson is about keeping a project coherent while its premise changes. The pivot affects the vehicles, the service pattern, the images, the budget and the decision gates at once. A coordination layer can carry that change across the working files; Michael still supplies the direction and decides what is worth pursuing.

01 / FRAME

Keep the question editable.

The useful moment is the pivot from a desirable object to a transport job. A studio needs enough room to change the brief after seeing the first version.

02 / COORDINATE

Give each thread a bounded job.

Research, operating logic, visual language and implementation need a common brief and a human review point. More outputs alone do not create agreement.

03 / TEST

Make the next spend answer a question.

A leased-shuttle trial would test demand before a hardware commitment. Its value would be a decision informed by riders, including a decision to stop.

04 / ACCOUNT

Keep the claim with its source.

A rendering is a rendering, a budget is an estimate, and a model ledger is an account of a process. None substitutes for a working service or a customer's commitment.

06 / PROJECT RECORD

An idea ready for a better question.

The weekend leaves a coherent proposal: a modular people mover, a service-hours thesis and a pilot that could test demand. The next useful work would bring that proposal into contact with prospective riders, buyers and operators.
