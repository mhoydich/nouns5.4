CREDIT CARD GAMES / A NEW PUBLIC ARCADE

Money becomes a multiplayer medium.

What if checkout did not end the experience? What if five dollars lit a window, planted a garden, opened a seat, added a note, or kept a public performance alive?

Concept and editorial direction: Michael Hoydich Made with Codex + Midjourney 12 minute read + live model
01 / THE PROVOCATION

A payment should make the room move.

The internet has made payment nearly frictionless and emotionally invisible. We tap, authorize, receive a receipt, and disappear. Credit Card Games starts with a stranger proposition: the contribution is the move.

A player puts in a small, clearly priced amount. The payment does not vanish behind a confirmation screen. It changes a shared public world. A window comes on. A chair unfolds. A flower opens. A band gains another minute. Everybody watching sees it happen at the same time.

This is neither a casino nor a prettier donation form. There is no financial prize, chance mechanic, mystery box, or whale who gets to erase the crowd. It is a category between multiplayer play, crowdfunding, public art, and creator software: a place where many modest acts accumulate into one extraordinary event.

THE MOVE IS A CONTRIBUTION.

THE SCORE IS SOMETHING REAL.

LIGHT THE BLOCK / NON-PAYMENT PREVIEW

One hundred dark rooms. One public outcome.

In the live game, each confirmed $5 move would light one window and attach a three-word dedication. The final window unlocks a commissioned performance, dinner, print run, or public object announced before play begins.

Preview only. No card form, charge, or stored payment information.

03 / THE TRUST LAYER

THE CONTRIBUTION CONTRACT

Wonder needs a boring, legible ledger.

Before a card appears, every game states the move, amount, target, beneficiary, deadline, outcome, and what happens if the target is missed. “Contribution” is never used as camouflage: if it is a purchase, creator payment, pre-order, or participation fee, the game says so.

YOUR MOVE

The exact public state that changes.

YOUR AMOUNT

The price, fees, and tax treatment.

THE GOAL

Confirmed moves or contribution volume required.

THE OUTCOME

The specific real thing that completion unlocks.

THE BENEFICIARY

Who receives money and in what capacity.

IF IT FALLS SHORT

Refund, extension, scaled result, or named alternative.

CHOOSE A MOVE→HOSTED CHECKOUT→ CONFIRMED EVENT→ONE LEDGER MOVE→ EVERY SCREEN CHANGES

THE PLATFORM THESIS

The billion-dollar version is not one hit game.

It is a system that lets thousands of artists, schools, venues, neighborhoods, publishers, musicians, organizers, and brands build their own contribution worlds. Credit Card Games supplies the live state, templates, receipts, payment-confirmation logic, fulfillment record, and beautiful public ending.

Crowdfunding taught people to back an outcome with a card. Multiplayer games taught them to expect a world to respond immediately. Creator software taught them to build without a platform engineer. Credit Card Games is where those expectations meet.

The moat is not card processing. It is the library of proven contribution mechanics, the public history of completed outcomes, and the distribution loop created when every player leaves with a beautiful receipt of the move they made.

04 / THE PATH

ONE WINDOW TO ONE BILLION

Four proofs, in order.

  1. PROOF 01 / $5K–$100K VOLUME

    Can one room feel electric?

    Ship Light the Block. Prove strangers understand it, contribute, stay to watch, share their room, and trust the final receipt. The first goal is not scale. It is a finished night people describe to someone else.

  2. PROOF 02 / $1M–$10M VOLUME

    Can creators make their own worlds?

    Productize four mechanics: finite build, time extension, collective authorship, and shared unlock. Charge for Studio only after organizers repeat without a custom production team.

  3. PROOF 03 / $10M–$100M VOLUME

    Can trust become distribution?

    Publish outcome receipts, fulfillment histories, contribution contracts, and reusable identities. Let a player in one finished game discover the next game from someone they already helped.

  4. PROOF 04 / $100M–$1B VOLUME

    Can the format become infrastructure?

    Expand through cultural institutions, schools, cities, publishers, global creator networks, and carefully chosen brands. Local visual worlds stay different; the ledger, templates, safety limits, and completion receipts become the platform.

NON-NEGOTIABLES

Scale the invitation, not the extraction.

  • One confirmed contribution creates one disclosed move.
  • No random financial prize or pay-to-win dominance.
  • Anonymous participation remains beautiful and complete.
  • Raw card details stay with the payment provider.
  • Refunded and disputed funds remain distinct from confirmed moves.
  • Every promised outcome receives a public completion receipt.

THE PITCH IN ONE BREATH

Credit Card Games turns checkout into a collective act: every contribution makes one beautiful public move, and when enough people play, something real begins.
