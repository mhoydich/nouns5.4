02 / DECISION RIGHTS

Own the system.
Yield the work.

The Product Manager makes choices legible and keeps commitments moving. The title does not create authority over craft, technical judgment, editorial voice, client promises, or a project lead’s domain.

PM OWNS

The operating surface.

  • Outcomes, priorities, backlog, and cadence
  • Decision briefs, evidence, and open questions
  • Dependencies, blockers, handoffs, and receipts
  • Stop, continue, and scale recommendations
  • A current public view of the work
LEADS OWN

The consequential call.

  • Product, creative, technical, and editorial judgment
  • Scope and quality inside their discipline
  • Approval before a public change in their domain
  • Who joins the work and how it is practiced
  • The right to revise the PM recommendation
FOUNDER RESOLVES

The portfolio boundary.

  • Money, equity, hiring, and external commitments
  • Brand, legal, safety, and reputation questions
  • Conflicts between accountable leads
  • Starting or ending a major workstream
  • Any exception to the published compensation gate

THE YIELD PROTOCOL

  1. 01

    Ask the accountable lead for the call.

  2. 02

    Record the decision and the evidence without rewriting the lead’s voice.

  3. 03

    Re-plan scope, dependencies, and timing around that call.

  4. 04

    Escalate only when money, legal risk, public safety, or two lead domains conflict.

  5. 05

    Never use process, urgency, or consensus theater to overrule craft.

03 / COMPENSATION GATE

No cash pay
before the trigger.

The Product Manager function is active now. A paid human engagement begins only after Industry Next closes financing or begins collecting revenue.

TRIGGER AA financing closes.
TRIGGER BCollected revenue begins.

A trigger starts a compensation conversation; it does not create retroactive salary, automatic equity, or guaranteed payment. Scope, rate, equity, classification, and effective date require a separate written agreement.

This page is an operating charter, not an employment offer. No candidate is expected to perform unpaid labor based on this page alone.

04 / WEEKLY RHYTHM

A small loop.
Real receipts.

  1. MONName the week.

    One outcome per active workstream. One lead. One next decision.

  2. WEDClear the path.

    Resolve blockers, expose drift, and return domain calls to the lead.

  3. FRIFile the receipt.

    Record what changed, what shipped, what was learned, and what stops.

  4. MONTHRebalance.

    Continue, revise, archive, or promote each project using verified behavior.
