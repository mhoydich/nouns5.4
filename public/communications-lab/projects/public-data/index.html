CL-07 / Public data / proposed

Public data with visible uncertainty

Can a small dashboard explain stale or missing public sensor readings honestly?

Educational project briefs · applications closed

These are proposed explorations for Mike, agents and future contributors. They are not internship openings or employment offers. Pay, hours, hiring entity, supervisor and privacy arrangements must be confirmed before any role opens. No application or work sample is requested here.

01 / One week

Make the smallest useful proof.

Choose one appropriately licensed public dataset; cache a snapshot and prototype current, stale, missing and suspect states.

02 / Four weeks

Stretch the experiment.

Compare two aggregation methods and publish reproducible transformations with attribution.

03 / Materials

Start with what is available.

  • $0–30; public dataset; existing laptop
  • Skills to practise: Data cleaning, chart design, basic statistics

Illustrative materials only. Equipment purchases, expenses and access need human review.

04 / Privacy + boundaries

Choose a safe test fixture.

Use public non-personal data only. No location inference about people and no emergency, health or safety guidance.

05 / Success

Measure the change.

Every chart shows unit, source and timestamp; all four failure states are visibly distinct

06 / Deliverable + review

Leave a usable handoff.

Dashboard prototype, license record and transformation notebook

Human review: Mentor approves dataset and uncertainty language. Mike approves publication; agents may summarize but cannot certify accuracy.

Before work enters the world.

  • Pay, hours, eligibility, supervisor, location, agreements and legal review must be resolved before recruitment or work begins. Budgets are illustrative materials estimates in USD, exclude labor, tax and shipping, and are not spending authorization.
  • Use synthetic or licensed public fixtures first. A human supervisor must approve any participant involvement, consent, collection and data lifecycle.
  • Agents may draft, code, propose tests and document evidence. They cannot supervise interns, approve their own work or infer authority to send messages, buy materials or publish.
  • PointCast publication is the programme’s intended context. Physical venues, partners and installations need separate confirmation; no external venue partnership is claimed.
  • Credit every contributor with permission. Publish measured results and limitations only after human review; preserve a record of corrections.
