Formula, capacity and exclusions

Break-even is the fixed budget divided by positive unit contribution, rounded up to whole units. Daily or subscriber break-even also divides by operating days or shipment cycles. If contribution is zero or negative, no finite positive-volume break-even exists.

The schedule is assumed fixed. Recalculate labor and equipment when demand crosses a capacity tier. Nothing here estimates a named business’s profit.

A café example you can inspect

An invented $7 ticket × 180 daily transactions × 26 days creates $32,760 in monthly net sales. A 30% variable-cost share leaves $4.90 contribution per transaction. After an invented $19,000 fixed labor and occupancy budget, the monthly remainder is $3,932, before exclusions. Whole-transaction break-even is 150/day.

      04 / A dated view of the world
      Context is evidence.
      It is not a forecast.
      Six official signals, observed October 3, 2026. Their reporting periods and geographies differ. Keep those differences visible.
      official published statistic
      U.S. retail coffee prices
      +6.1% year over year
      August 2025 to August 2026
      U.S. city average
      The national coffee CPI-U category increased 6.1% over twelve months. Its August month-to-month change was −0.9% before seasonal adjustment.
      What this cannot tell us
      It is not a café menu-price index, wholesale bean quote, dollar price per pound, local demand measure or shop margin.

      official published statistic
      Los Angeles food service price backdrop
      +2.4% year over year
      August 2025 to August 2026
      Los Angeles–Long Beach–Anaheim; Los Angeles and Orange counties
      The area's food-away-from-home CPI-U increased 2.4% over twelve months and was unchanged from July to August.
      What this cannot tell us
      The metro index is broader than coffee, is not seasonally adjusted, and has a smaller sample than the national CPI. It does not measure an El Segundo café or compare price levels between cities.

      official published rule
      California general wage floor
      $16.90/hour
      Effective January 1, 2026
      California
      California's general minimum wage is $16.90 per hour for employers outside a higher industry or local wage requirement.
      What this cannot tell us
      Some localities and industries have higher requirements. Do not apply this general floor to every café or chain; verify the workplace and applicable rules. The calculator’s $19,000 fixed budget is an invented teaching assumption, not a statutory wage or reported shop payroll.

      official published estimate
      El Segundo resident context
      16,379 residents
      July 1, 2025 estimate (V2025); income period 2020–2024
      El Segundo city, California
      Census estimates 16,379 city residents on July 1, 2025. The 2020–2024 median household income is $150,737 in 2024 dollars.
      What this cannot tell us
      City residents are not the population of the 25-mile atlas. Household income is not individual purchasing power; neither figure counts café customers, commuters, airport visitors or daytime workers. Five-year income estimates are historical averages.

      official forecast
      Global supply outlook
      189.7m / 179.7m bags
      USDA July 2026 forecast for 2026/27

      USDA forecasts 2026/27 world coffee production at 189.7 million bags and consumption at 179.7 million bags; each bag is 60 kilograms.

      Forecasts can change. Marketing-year calendars vary by country. Production minus consumption is not a shop profit, delivered inventory forecast or complete stocks/trade accounting.
