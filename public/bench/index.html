Read the whole minute

Arrive. Look at the bench as it is. Tools, cups, open tabs. You do not have to fix any of it yet.

Notice. Pick up one thing in your mind and set it where it goes. Then another. The bench gets wider as you watch.

Stay. Hold the one thing you mean to make today. Just that one. Let the rest wait on the shelf.

Return. Lay your hands flat on the desk. Begin with the smallest true step. Leave the bench as clean as you found it.

One of four quiet places across our sites. It borrows an idea from Japanese craft care, where a building is rebuilt on a cycle so the skill is kept by doing. Drawn in code; no photographs.
