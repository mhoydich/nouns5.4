01 / THE DESK

What an AI buying desk does.

One kitchen, or a few kitchens pooled together, buying from what's actually inside 25 miles: certified farmers markets, the downtown wholesale produce district, the ports, and the distributors already serving the South Bay.

AGENTS DO
Forecast, compare, route, flag waste, write receipts
PEOPLE DO
Sign, set limits, pick growers, decide who goes short
EVERYONE GETS
A receipt per order a person can read in a minute
THE DESK / FIVE JOBS 5 JOBS
  1. ✓
    Forecast

    Turn a menu, a headcount and the season into a weekly order, with how sure it is.

  2. ✓
    Source

    Compare price, distance, freshness and the grower's terms, and keep small nearby growers in the running.

  3. ✓
    Pool

    Let willing kitchens buy one staple together on one delivery loop, so a small grower can sell a full truck.

  4. ✓
    Move and recover

    Plan the route, use the empty trip for crates and compost, and book surplus for a pantry before it spoils.

  5. ✓
    Show its work

    Publish a plain-language receipt for every order that a cook, a parent or a grower could overrule.

WHAT THE DESK NEVER DOES ALONE: sign a contract, spend past a set limit, add or drop a grower, set what a grower is paid, approve an allergy or diet substitution, act on a food-safety hold, or decide who eats when there isn't enough.

02 / WHY IT'S NOT A FANTASY

Big buyers already
changed what "local" means.

01

A RULEBOOK EXISTS

Good Food Purchasing.

The City of Los Angeles and LA Unified adopted the Good Food Purchasing Program in 2012, a published standard for buying local, fair and healthy food.

GOODFOODPURCHASING.ORG ↗
02

IT MOVED THE NUMBER

10% to 50–72%.

The program reports LAUSD produce sourced within 200 miles went from about 10 percent to 50 to 72 percent, depending on the season.

PROGRAM'S OWN REPORT ↗
03

THE LAW PUSHES RECOVERY

SB 1383.

California's food-recovery rules ask large grocers, wholesale markets and distributors to donate edible surplus, with a statewide goal of recovering at least 20 percent.

CITY SB 1383 PAGE ↗
04

THE GAP IS SMALL KITCHENS

Nobody pools them.

A district can hire a buying team. An office café, a pantry and a restaurant group can't, and that's where an agent desk with person sign-off earns its keep.

03 / THE FIRST PILOT

One staple.
Three kitchens.
One season.

  1. 01
    Find three willing kitchens.

    For example a school, an office cafeteria and a pantry inside the 25 miles, each with one accountable buyer.

  2. 02
    Pick one staple and its growers.

    The buyers choose the growers and the terms. The desk only drafts.

  3. 03
    Run it in the open.

    Every order gets a public receipt with the private details taken out, and the buyers sign each week.

  4. 04
    Publish the result, good or bad.

    Price, waste, grower payout timing and time saved, side by side with how the kitchens bought before.
