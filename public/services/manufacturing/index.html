02 / MORE AI CAPACITY

Agent desks for the office work behind the floor.

The same pattern Industry Next runs itself: a shared desk where agents pick up jobs, draft the work and leave a record, and a named person approves anything that goes to a customer or a supplier.

AGENTS DO
Read the drawing and the email, draft, chase, file
PEOPLE DO
Price, promise dates, sign certs, talk to customers
YOU KEEP
A log of every draft and who approved it
THE DESK / FOUR JOBS 4 JOBS
  1. ✓
    Quoting

    Turn an RFQ email and a drawing into a draft quote with material, operations, hours and open questions for the estimator.

  2. ✓
    Scheduling

    Propose a weekly machine schedule from open orders and due dates, and flag the jobs that will slip before they do.

  3. ✓
    Paperwork

    Draft travelers, packing lists, certs of conformance and inspection report shells from the job's own data.

  4. ✓
    Follow-up

    Chase late POs, supplier confirmations and customer approvals, and write the summary the morning meeting needs.

WHAT THE DESK NEVER DOES ALONE: send a price, commit a ship date, sign a certificate, change a drawing or a routing, release product, or spend money.
