# Industry Next Product Manager

Status: active operating function  
Public desk: <https://www.industrynext.xyz/product/>  
Machine-readable state: <https://www.industrynext.xyz/product.json>

## Mission

Turn signals into decisions and shipped proofs without taking the work away
from its accountable leads.

## Working contract

The Product Manager owns the operating surface: outcomes, priorities, backlog,
cadence, evidence, decision briefs, dependencies, blockers, handoffs, receipts,
and stop / continue / scale recommendations.

The Product Manager does not own product, creative, technical, editorial, or
client judgment inside an accountable lead's domain. The lead makes the
consequential call. The PM records it, replans around it, and escalates only
when money, legal risk, public safety, or two lead domains conflict.

The founder resolves portfolio priority, money, equity, hiring, external
commitments, legal and reputation questions, and conflicts between leads.

## Compensation gate

Current cash compensation is $0. A paid human engagement may begin only after
Industry Next closes financing or begins collecting revenue. A trigger begins
a compensation conversation; it does not create retroactive salary, automatic
equity, or guaranteed payment. Scope, rate, equity, classification, and
effective date require a separate written agreement.

This is an operating charter, not an employment offer. No candidate is expected
to perform unpaid labor based on this document.

## Weekly cadence

- Monday: one outcome, one accountable lead, and one next decision for every
  active workstream.
- Wednesday: clear blockers, expose drift, and return domain calls to the lead.
- Friday: record what changed, shipped, was learned, and stops.
- Monthly: continue, revise, archive, or promote each project using verified
  behavior.

## Update contract

`public/product.json` is the structured operating state. The public HTML is the
human surface. `/product/` is the public portfolio truth; `/desk/` is a
device-local task execution tool. Local task activity does not become public
status by implication. Update the HTML and JSON together, update `updated_at`,
then run `npm run verify:product-desk`.

Never present impressions as people, drafts as releases, activity as progress,
or a PM recommendation as a lead decision. Every public status must have a
verifiable receipt or be labeled as a hypothesis, gate, or next decision.
